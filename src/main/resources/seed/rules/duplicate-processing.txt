NEXA Card Scheme - Dispute Rule P40
Duplicate Processing
Rulebook version 2026.1. NEXA is a fictional card scheme used for demonstration only.

Section P40.1 Definition
Reason code P40 applies when a single purchase was processed more than once: the same card, the same merchant and the same amount, posted on the same day or within 3 days, when the cardholder took part in only one purchase.

Section P40.2 Eligibility conditions
P40 may be raised only when all of the following are true:
- Two or more transactions share the card, merchant and amount.
- The transactions were processed within 3 calendar days of each other.
- The cardholder confirms that only one purchase was made.

Section P40.3 Invalid uses
P40 must not be raised when any of the following apply:
- The transactions represent separate purchases and the cardholder received goods or services for each.
- The charges are separate instalments or recurring payments agreed with the merchant.
- The merchant has already refunded the duplicate transaction.

Section P40.4 Required issuer evidence
- References of both the original and the duplicate transaction
- Cardholder statement confirming that only one purchase was made

Section P40.5 Time limit
The dispute must be raised within 120 calendar days of the processing date of the duplicate transaction.
