NEXA Card Scheme - Dispute Rule C50
Credit Not Processed
Rulebook version 2026.1. NEXA is a fictional card scheme used for demonstration only.

Section C50.1 Definition
Reason code C50 applies when the merchant agreed to a refund, or accepted a cancellation within its published cancellation policy, but the credit did not reach the cardholder's account.

Section C50.2 Eligibility conditions
C50 may be raised only when all of the following are true:
- The merchant issued a credit voucher, a refund confirmation or a cancellation number, or the cancellation was made within the merchant's published policy.
- At least 15 calendar days have passed since the refund was promised or the cancellation was made.
- No credit for the transaction has posted to the cardholder's account.

Section C50.3 Invalid uses
C50 must not be raised when any of the following apply:
- A refund for the transaction has already posted to the account.
- The cancellation was made outside the merchant's published policy and the merchant did not agree to a refund.

Section C50.4 Required issuer evidence
- Credit voucher, refund confirmation or cancellation confirmation from the merchant
- Cancellation number or date of cancellation
- Cardholder statement confirming that no credit has been received

Section C50.5 Time limit
The dispute must be raised within 120 calendar days of the date on the credit voucher or of the cancellation.
