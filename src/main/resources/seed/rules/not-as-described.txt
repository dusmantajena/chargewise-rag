NEXA Card Scheme - Dispute Rule Q30
Not as Described or Defective
Rulebook version 2026.1. NEXA is a fictional card scheme used for demonstration only.

Section Q30.1 Definition
Reason code Q30 applies when the goods were received but differ significantly from the merchant's description, or arrived damaged or defective.

Section Q30.2 Eligibility conditions
Q30 may be raised only when all of the following are true:
- The goods were delivered to the cardholder.
- The goods differ materially from the description shown at the time of purchase, or are damaged or defective.
- The cardholder returned the goods, or attempted to return them, following the merchant's return instructions.

Section Q30.3 Invalid uses
Q30 must not be raised when any of the following apply:
- The cardholder simply changed their mind or no longer wants the goods.
- The merchant has already refunded the transaction.
- The cardholder did not return or attempt to return the goods.

Section Q30.4 Required issuer evidence
- Cardholder statement explaining how the goods differ from the description
- Copy of the merchant's product description or listing
- Proof of return or attempted return, such as a tracking reference
- Photographs of the goods where available

Section Q30.5 Time limit
The dispute must be raised within 120 calendar days of the date the goods were received.
